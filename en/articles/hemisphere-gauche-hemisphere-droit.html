Left hemisphere, right hemisphere

Provisional English translation — awaiting review and approval.

Réal Genest Sensei — Originally published: 2017-11-03

Dr. Jill Bolte Taylor is a neuroanatomist at Harvard University. In her book My Stroke of Insight, which I invite you to read, she recounts the events surrounding her stroke — a cerebrovascular accident — in the left hemisphere, and how she experienced the right hemisphere.

Understand that this is not a scientific presentation of neurological theories that make academics shiver with pleasure, but an academic in neurology describing her experience: perceiving the world through a damaged left hemisphere, and the influence of the Right Hemisphere on how reality is interpreted. We must understand that, generally, we do not have direct access to our Right Hemisphere (RH).

It must be accessed indirectly: by visualizing symbols and images, associating symbols or images with a voluntary action, or using virtual means. Meditation and relaxation methods give great importance to techniques that allow the Right Hemisphere to manifest consciously as intuitions. The aim is to “shut up” the left hemisphere, which shouts at the top of its voice and crushes the small voice of intuition.

In “silence”, the right hemisphere listens to the environment and the Universe.

In a recorded video presentation, Dr. Jill Bolte Taylor presents the subject of cerebral asymmetry. She asserts that the two cerebral hemispheres are completely separate physically (but linked by a bridge, the corpus callosum), and process different subjects in different ways, such that they appear to have distinct “personalities”:

Video of Dr. Jill Bolte Taylor

The “right brain” would function like a parallel processor (processing all information simultaneously), operating in the “here and now”. It would translate into images (see also visual thinking) and learn “kinesthetically” through body movements. It would instantly manage and associate the overall feeling of the senses: sounds, smells, images, the body's position in space, etc.

Functions of the right hemisphere:

• Feminine

• Intuition

• Unconscious

• Synthesis

• Creativity

• Uses emotions

• Energies activated by energies

• Oriented toward the big picture

• Imagination takes precedence

• Symbols and images

• The present moment

• Philosophy and religion

• Spontaneity

• Here and now

• Believes

• Knows the function of objects

• Impetuous

• Willingness to take risks

• Sensory feeling

The “left brain” would function like a serial processor (processing information sequentially). It would think linearly and comparatively, particularly in time. Its purpose would be to extract details from the present moment to categorize and organize them, comparing them with past events to project future possibilities (see also abstraction). It would translate into language and manage the separation and distinction of things.

It would give rise to the feeling of being who we are, distinct from others, which she says is the most important thing she lost during her stroke (which affected the brain's left side).

Functions of the left hemisphere:

• Masculine

• Uses logic

• Conscious

• Memorization

• Detail-oriented

• Analytical thinking

• A tendency to structure

• Time is linear

• Facts take precedence

• Words and languages

• Present and past

• Mathematics and sciences

• Knows

• Knows the names of objects

• Based on realities

• Practical

• Security

The concept of LH and RH is not presented as an absolute scientific truth. It seems this view gives fundamentalist academics tapeworms. According to these Imams, anything absent from science's sacred texts has no right to exist. Lived experience is of no interest!

This concept is only a tool to facilitate understanding of the mechanisms of feeling and intellectual knowledge… It is like tasting wine: I can take a few drops, put them in a device that will analyse the precious liquid and print a long list of molecules and chemical substances. Science will be well served. Yet “You have no opinion until you have tasted!” — Max Léglise. The essence of wine reveals itself when the nectar enters the mouth.

Only then do you become acquainted with its smells, aromas and taste sensations.

With all due respect to sceptical fundamentalists, some dimensions of life escape test tubes and laboratory instruments. That is a good thing, and what gives Life its flavour. I do not need to know the composition of the elements from which a musical instrument is constructed. A guitar's purpose is to resonate beneath the artist's fingers and convey emotions.

The essential lies not in the instrument's construction, but in the sensation, rhythm and sensitivity guiding the artist's touch. An actor on stage combines both hemispheres' activities. They take possession of a role by studying the character's history and behaviour. All memorization of the text belongs to LH. If the performer is a true actor, they will create their character.

The more the actor's ego tends to vanish, the more the character takes shape, and the more credible that created character becomes. We are in RH. When the character comes to life and a current of energy flows between actors and spectators, we are also in RH.

When I look at a painting by Vincent Van Gogh, I can analyse the artist's technique and know everything about the painting's history, but if I confine myself to a logical, rational view, I have completely missed the work's human dimension. To appreciate it, we must contemplate, absorb and feel. We must let the right hemisphere do its work.

Finally, if you attend a classical music concert, to benefit fully from that time you must disconnect your big left brain and become receptive (RH) to the vibrations emitted by the musicians. If you do not understand these simple examples, go back to talking to your computer! When approaching spirituality, we centre spiritual experience in the Right Hemisphere. In Zen, the experience of “satori” belongs to RH.

Training techniques and methods aim to encourage an awareness triggered in RH: grasping reality in the present moment, without an analytical process. If I walk down the street and feel someone is following me, that feeling or sense is triggered in RH.

The same applies when I must imagine or visualize a scene, a situation or a ritual. To understand and assess the relevance of the ritual opening or closing a Kobudo training session, we must consider the roles of the right and left hemispheres and the mechanisms activated as the ritual unfolds.

Kobudō requires practitioners to put themselves in a scene, to visualize a critical situation in which life and death stand side by side; they must manage the situation while avoiding, if possible, killing their opponent. From the moment their eyes meet and the aggressor clearly reveals an intention to fight, the defender must draw upon intuition, feeling and the ability to prevail while avoiding the trap of “Satsujin no ken (the sword that gives death)”.

Kobudō centres on the development of individual consciousness and places technical prowess last. Because of their culture, Westerners find it extremely difficult to grasp the profound meaning of Awaseru 合わせる. A sound understanding of the right hemisphere's work is needed before claiming knowledge of Kobudō.

Japanese masters often say: “Gaikoku no hitobito wa kobudo no Kokoro Orikaii suru no ga mutsukashi yo u desu (For most foreigners, the spirit of Kobudo is difficult to grasp and understand).” Here, Kokoro does not mean academic or technical discourse, but the deep consciousness linking humanity to the Universe. This outlook is almost always scorned by the ignorant “gaijin”. Such a contemptuous attitude causes masters to avoid foreigners.

An individual's intelligence cannot be refreshed when it lets its excess of ignorance overflow.

The few “gaijin” accepted by masters in the past cherish deep within themselves the privilege they enjoyed. In classical Kobudō dojo circles, it is said that a master's teaching is transmitted from the master's heart to the disciple's heart: “sensei no kokoro kara, deshi no kokoro made”. ― Réal Genest Sensei
