FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Interested in discovering iaidō or jōdō? Here are some answers to help you prepare for your first visit to our dojo.

01 /Can I start without any martial arts experience?

Yes. No previous experience is needed to discover iaidō or jōdō. Beginners are welcome and progress at their own pace.

02 /What is the difference between iaidō and jōdō?

Iaidō, as taught in the Musō Shinden Ryū school, is a Japanese martial art focused on swordsmanship. It is practised mainly alone, through kata that incorporate drawing the sword, cutting and returning the sword to its scabbard.

Jōdō, as taught in the Shindō Musō Ryū school, is a Japanese martial art practised in pairs, using a wooden staff (jō) against a wooden sword (bokken). The kata develop control of distance, timing and movement.

Visit our Iaido and Jodo pages to learn more.

03 /Where does training take place?

Training takes place in Montreal, at Proto Studio in the Mile End and at the Collège de Rosemont sports centre. The location of each session can be confirmed before your first visit.

04 /When are classes held?

Training sessions are held on Saturdays from 9 a.m. to noon and on Tuesdays from 7:30 to 9 p.m. The schedule may occasionally vary.

05 /What equipment do I need to get started?

No special equipment is needed for your first training session. We recommend wearing sportswear that allows you to move freely.

We have equipment available to borrow: a jō (wooden staff), a bokken (wooden sword) and an iaitō (unsharpened training sword). This lets you become familiar with the practice before buying your own equipment.

06 /Can I attend a class before joining?

You are welcome to observe a class—and even better, to take part—before joining. A first visit can be arranged on request.

07 /How much does membership cost?

Membership fees are paid every three months and are used primarily to cover the cost of renting training spaces. The dojo operates year-round, and you can expect to pay between $60 and $100 per month, depending on the number of members. The exact fees will be confirmed before you join.

08 /How do I join Sandokai?

Write to us to let us know you’re interested. We’ll explain how to attend your first training session and begin your practice. Contact us.
