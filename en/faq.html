FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Interested in discovering iaidō or jōdō? Here are some answers to help you prepare for your first visit to our dojo.

01 /Can I join without previous martial arts experience?

Absolutely. No previous experience is required to begin learning iaidō or jōdō. Beginners are welcome, and everyone is encouraged to progress at their own pace.

02 /What is the difference between iaidō and jōdō?

Iaidō is a traditional Japanese martial art focused on the use of the sword, practised through structured sequences of movements. Jōdō centres on the jō, a wooden staff, with training primarily involving paired exercises. Explore our Iaido and Jodo pages.

03 /Where do classes take place?

Training takes place at Collège de Rosemont in Montreal’s Rosemont–La Petite-Patrie neighbourhood. Please contact us to confirm the location before your first visit.

04 /When are classes held?

According to the previously published schedule, classes take place on Saturdays from 9:30 a.m. to noon, from September through June. Please contact us before your first visit to confirm the schedule.

05 /What should I bring to my first class?

Get in touch before attending your first session. We’ll let you know what to wear and whether training equipment can be borrowed.

06 /Can I visit before joining?

You’re welcome to contact us to arrange a first visit and learn about opportunities to participate.

07 /How much does membership cost?

Please contact our dojo for current membership fees and any additional costs.

08 /How do I join Sandokai?

Simply reach out and let us know you’re interested. We’ll help you arrange your first visit and explain how to get started. Contact us.
