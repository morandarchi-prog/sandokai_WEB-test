Musō Shinden Ryū

Iaido

The way of the sword

A sword practice bringing together precision of movement and self-control.

01 /

Movement and mindset

Iaido is a Japanese art of drawing the sword. Training seeks precise movement and a non-aggressive attitude.

02 /

Practising kata

Sequences are mostly performed alone, standing or kneeling. They combine drawing, cutting, chiburi and returning the sword to its scabbard.

03 /

Progressive learning

Musō Shinden Ryū includes three series: Shoden, Chuden and Okuden. Study develops awareness, perception and a sincere attitude.
